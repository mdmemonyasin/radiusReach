Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how radiusReach handles the information you share with us. Replace with your final legal copy before launch.

What we collect

When you contact us through the enquiry form, WhatsApp, phone or email, we collect the details you choose to give us — such as your name, phone/WhatsApp number, business name and your message.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and recommend the right next step.
  • To deliver the services you ask us to provide.
  • To keep you updated about your campaigns and results.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't send spam. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Data retention

We keep your enquiry details only as long as needed to help you or to meet legal obligations, then remove them.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing us. We'll respond promptly.

Cookies & analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website — for example which pages are viewed and how people arrive. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects anonymised usage data (such as an approximate location and device type); it does not tell us who you are personally. You can opt out using Google's browser opt-out add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email admin@radiusreach.in or message us on WhatsApp.
